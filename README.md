# 🛒 Blinkit Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and outlet-level business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Identify top-performing product categories and items.
* Compare outlet performance.
* Analyze sales based on outlet type and location.
* Study the impact of item characteristics on sales.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing outlets.
* Analyze outlet age and operational performance.
* Create an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report contains multiple analytical pages.

### 📄 Page 1 — Sales Overview

The Sales Overview page provides a high-level summary of business performance.

Key information includes:

* Total Sales
* Total Items
* Average Sales
* Average Rating
* Sales by Outlet Type
* Sales by Item Type
* Sales by Outlet Location
* Overall sales distribution

This page is designed for quick business performance monitoring.

---

### 📄 Page 2 — Outlet Analysis

The Outlet Analysis page focuses on outlet-level performance.

The analysis includes:

* Sales by Outlet Type
* Sales by Outlet Size
* Sales by Location
* Outlet Establishment Year
* Years of Operation
* Outlet performance comparison

This helps identify which outlet types and locations contribute the most to overall sales.

---

### 📄 Page 3 — Product Performance

This page focuses on product-level analysis.

The dashboard analyzes:

* Top-performing Item Types
* Sales by Item Type
* Item Fat Content
* Item Visibility
* Item Weight
* Item Rating
* Product performance comparison

A **Top 10 Item Types** analysis is also included to identify the most valuable product categories.

---

### 📄 Page 4 — Customer Insights & Business Insights

The final dashboard page focuses on combining customer and business perspectives.

Key analysis includes:

* Customer purchasing patterns
* Product preferences
* Outlet performance
* Sales trends
* High-performing outlets
* Low-performing outlets
* Business opportunities

The page provides actionable insights that can help management make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🔎 Drill-Through Analysis

The report includes drill-through functionality to move from high-level performance to detailed outlet analysis.

The drill-through hierarchy follows:

```text
Tier
  ↓
Outlet Type
  ↓
Outlet
  ↓
Item
```

This allows users to investigate specific business segments and understand the factors affecting their performance.
